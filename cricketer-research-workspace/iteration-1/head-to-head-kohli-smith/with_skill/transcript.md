# Final report from the with_skill run (Kohli vs Smith)

Wrote the Kohli vs Smith comparison and saved dossier.md and user_notes.md. ESPNcricinfo, Statsguru, HowSTAT and Cricbuzz all refused automated access, so the figures rest on Wikipedia, two undated stats sites and search excerpts.

Verdict: Steve Smith is the better Test batter by a clear margin. Kohli is the better batter across formats, also by a clear margin.
- Tests: Smith averages 55.29 to Kohli's 46.85 over a similar number of Tests (127 v 123), with 37 hundreds to 30.
- Home and away: Smith's average drops about 5 runs away from home; Kohli's drops about 14.
- England: Smith averages 58.51 there, Kohli 33.21.
- What decided it: Kohli averaged about 30 from 2020 to 2024, while Smith kept his level for roughly a decade. ICC named Smith Test Cricketer of the Decade 2011–2020 and Kohli overall and ODI Cricketer of the Decade.
- When it flips: with no format named, Kohli wins easily (ODI average 59.07, record 55 hundreds; T20I average 48.69). It narrows if only 2016–2019 is compared or if captaincy counts.
- Ratings: level at 66 each overall, but 27–21 to Smith on the three Test-specific rows.
